Sloppy Tanks support

Pick a tank, a map and a difficulty in Battle Setup and fight bots for the top score. Cover breaks, drums explode and supply crates repair your tank or load special ammunition.

Controls

On iPhone and iPad, the left stick drives, touching the arena aims and fires, the mine button lays a mine and tapping a round switches ammunition. On a Mac or PC, drive with WASD, aim with the mouse, hold the left button to fire, right-click to lay a mine and switch ammunition with Q and E. Pause the game to see every control.

Something wrong?

Write to support@sloppytanks.com with your device, its system version and what happened. The same address takes reports about a player's behaviour in an online room; include the room code.
