Sloppy Tanks privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Sloppy Tanks is a tank game for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and the web. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not collect any.

What stays on your device

Your settings (tank, map, difficulty, graphics, sound, controls), your player name and your personal bests are saved on your device only: in the app's own storage, or in your browser's local storage for the web game. Deleting the app or clearing the site's data removes them.

Multiplayer

Single player works offline and sends nothing. When you play online, the game connects to our game servers and sends what the room needs to run: your player name, your choices for the room and your controls while you play. Other players in the same room see your name, your tank and your score. A room and everything in it are kept in memory only while it runs and are gone once it ends; the servers keep no player names and write no logs of who played.

Your player name is two words the game draws from a fixed list. In the web game and the Windows app you may type a name of your own instead, up to 21 letters, digits and a few punctuation marks; it is the only text you write that other players see, so leave your real name and anything personal out of it. The iPhone, iPad and Mac apps only draw names, and they play on servers of their own that show no name a player typed. The game has no chat.

The list of open rooms shows each room's code, map, difficulty, length and player count, but no names. Like any internet service, the servers and our hosting providers (Cloudflare and Vultr) see your IP address while you are connected; we do not store it or use it to identify you.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Nobody types a message that other players read. In the iPhone, iPad and Mac apps nobody types a name either: every name there comes from a fixed list of words. In the web game and the Windows app a player may type a short name, which the others in the room see until the room ends.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions, or a report about a player: support@sloppytanks.com.
