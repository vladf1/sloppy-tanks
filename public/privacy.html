Sloppy Tanks privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Sloppy Tanks is a tank game for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and the web. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data. Multiplayer uses the information described below, and browser diagnostic uploads are opt-in.

What stays on your device

Your settings (tank, map, difficulty, graphics, sound, controls), your player name and your personal bests are saved on your device, in the app's own storage or in your browser. Clearing the app or site data removes those local copies. The web game also caches generated models and scenery in your browser.

Multiplayer

Single player runs on your device. The web game downloads its files to start and load maps. When you open Multiplayer, the game contacts the directory to list servers and rooms. If you join a room, the game sends your player name, room choices and controls to the game server. Other players in the same room see your name, your tank and your score. Room state and player names are held in memory until the room ends. Operational logs record room codes, connection events and network performance. They omit player names.

Your player name is two words the game draws from a fixed list. In the web game and the Windows app you may type a name of your own instead, up to 21 letters, digits and a few punctuation marks; it is the only text you write that other players see, so leave your real name and anything personal out of it. The iPhone, iPad and Mac apps only draw names, and they play on servers of their own that show no name a player typed. The game has no chat.

The list of open rooms shows each room's code, map, difficulty, length and player count, but no names. Like any internet service, our servers and hosting providers (Cloudflare and Vultr) receive your IP address while you are connected. The game server uses it only to limit request rates; we do not store it or use it to identify you.

Optional browser diagnostics

Adding ?trace to the game page address turns on diagnostic uploads for that browser. The uploads can include the page and connection URLs (including room codes), your browser and device details, graphics activity and errors. Recent notes are saved in your browser so they can be uploaded after a crash. The server directory makes the latest 40 uploaded traces publicly readable. Adding ?trace=off stops future uploads. Clearing the site data removes the local notes.

Children

Nobody types a message that other players read. The iPhone, iPad and Mac apps assign names from fixed word lists. The web and Windows editions allow short names that others in the room can see. Players should leave real names and other personal information out of those names.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions, or a report about a player: support@sloppytanks.com.
