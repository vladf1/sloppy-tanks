Sloppy Tanks privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Sloppy Tanks is a tank game for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and the web. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not collect any.

What stays on your device

Your settings (tank, map, difficulty, graphics, sound, controls) and your personal bests are saved on your device only: in the app's own storage, or in your browser's local storage for the web game. Deleting the app or clearing the site's data removes them.

Multiplayer

Single player works offline and sends nothing. When you play online, the game connects to our game servers and sends what the room needs to run: the player name you type, your choices for the room and your controls while you play. Other players in the same room see your name, your tank and your score. A room and everything in it are kept in memory only while it runs and are gone once it ends; the servers keep no player names and write no logs of who played.

The list of open rooms shows each room's code, map, difficulty, length and player count, but no names. Like any internet service, the servers and our hosting providers (Cloudflare and Vultr) see your IP address while you are connected; we do not store it or use it to identify you.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Player names in online rooms are checked for offensive words.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions, or a report about a player: support@sloppytanks.com.
