Previous build measurements. The timing and longevity results below predate the larger arena and visual redesign. They have not been rerun for this version.

What these numbers mean

Chrome renders at 2560 × 1440 physical pixels, with one shadow-casting sun, a 60 Hz physics step and 12 live tank slots. The first five seconds of each timing sample are excluded. Frame time includes browser scheduling; simulation and render CPU submission times are measured separately.

Normal target: sustained 60 FPS, p95 ≤ 20 ms, p99 ≤ 33 ms. Stress repeatedly injects 24 tanks, 200 projectiles, 150 physical fragments and drum chains; these are diagnostic loads. Live counts decrease as projectiles collide and fragments expire. Longevity uses real elapsed time and automatic completed-match restarts. Garbage collection is requested for comparable retained-memory snapshots.
